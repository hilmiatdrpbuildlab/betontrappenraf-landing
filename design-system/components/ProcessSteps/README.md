# ProcessSteps

The way a cast-in-place stair is made, in four steps drawn as a rising flight of treads: each step sits one riser higher than the one before, with a red tread line on top.

## Use
- `ol.rg-steps` > four `li.rg-step`, each with `span.rg-step__num` (**01** to **04**), an icon, an `h3.t-h3` and one sentence.
- The rise is automatic for four items (`--rise` 3, 2, 1, 0 × 56px). With three items set `--rise` inline. Under 900px the steps stack with a red rail on the left.
- Best on Dark. On Light it works unchanged.
- The numbers are real here: they are the order of the work. Do not number lists that are not a sequence.
- Icons: `ruler`, `hammer`, `layers`, `check`.

## Content
The four steps and sentences describe how a stair is cast in place (opmeten, bekisten, wapenen en storten, ontkisten). Check the wording with Raf, and add his own details such as how long the concrete cures before stripping, only as he states them.
